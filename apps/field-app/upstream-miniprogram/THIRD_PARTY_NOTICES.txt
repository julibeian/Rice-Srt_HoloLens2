In-report symptom comparison photographs (assets/symptoms/):
- LSU AgCenter / Donald Groth: rice blast, brown spot, sheath blight,
  and bacterial panicle blight photographs.
- Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU): bacterial leaf streak,
  bacterial leaf blight, tungro, dead-heart and bore-hole photographs.
- Ishikawa Prefecture Agricultural Experiment Station: rice downy-mildew
  photographs (Sclerophthora macrospora; 黄化萎縮病 on the source page).
The photographs remain the property of their institutions/authors.
Original page URLs, image URLs and source verification hashes
are recorded in docs/symptom-photo-sources.json. Seventeen existing cached
JPEGs are reused unchanged; the bore-hole photograph is an unchanged
JPEG from TNAU. The complete earlier source archive is retained in
_archive/knowledge/assets/README.md. Attribution does not grant a new
license or imply institutional endorsement.

The coordinate conversion mathematics in `utils/coordinates.js` is adapted
from [wandergis/coordtransform](https://github.com/wandergis/coordtransform).
The iterative inverse, validation and location integration are local additions.
